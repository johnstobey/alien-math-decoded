octave equivalence as the identity element. The Tonnetz torus is the fold made

### Kalachakra: 3/5
The Kalachakra tantra's 12-part time wheel is the most spatially explicit

corpus. The declaration of the ring's topology is unambiguous. Weaknesses:
the approach-without-closure feature is only partially attested, and Feature 5
(anamnesis) is absent.
